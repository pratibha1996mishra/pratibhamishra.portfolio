Project 01 · Pharma / Compliance
Compliance Simulation & Workflow Learning Program
01 Executive Summary
▾
A leading pharmaceutical organization required a scalable compliance training solution to ensure 1,000+ employees met regulatory requirements. The program replaced static PDF-based training with immersive SCORM-compliant simulations, branching decision scenarios, and LMS-tracked assessments — reducing compliance risk and improving audit-readiness across business units.
02Business Challenge
▾
Compliance training completion rates were below 68%, audit findings cited inadequate documentation of training workflows, and manual tracking created reporting blind spots for compliance managers. Regulatory non-compliance risk was elevated across three business units.
03Learning Challenge
▾
Learners lacked practical decision-making skills for real-world compliance scenarios. Role-specific knowledge gaps existed between frontline workers and managers. Existing content failed to contextualize regulations within actual workflows.
04Stakeholder Ecosystem
▾
L&D Team Operations HR Business Leaders Compliance Teams QA Managers
05Learning Strategy
▾
Applied ADDIE + Backward Design: defined compliance outcomes first, then mapped branching decision scenarios using Bloom's Taxonomy (Apply, Evaluate levels). Designed 15+ SCORM modules with Articulate Storyline 360 featuring situational simulations, try-mode interactions, and scenario-based assessments. WCAG 2.1/2.2 accessibility compliance built in from storyboard stage.
06LMS & Reporting Ecosystem
▾
Modules deployed via SCORM 1.2 to enterprise LMS. xAPI statements tracked attempt counts, completion timestamps, and branching paths chosen. LMS reporting dashboards surfaced completion by role and department. Power BI connected to LMS data exports for executive reporting views.
07Operational Outcomes
▾
Compliance training completion rate improved from 68% to 94%. Assessment pass rates increased by 22 percentage points. Review cycle time reduced by 30% through structured SME feedback workflows. Zero critical audit findings related to training documentation in subsequent audit cycle.

Project 03 · Digital Adoption / WBT
Workflow Learning & Digital Adoption Program
01Executive Summary
▾
Designed a workflow-embedded learning program using SAP Enable Now and Whatfix to accelerate enterprise software adoption across HR and Operations functions. The program delivered contextual, in-app guidance overlaid directly on live systems — reducing reliance on classroom training and enabling self-sufficient, just-in-time learning at the point of need.
02Business Challenge
▾
New ERP system rollout was accompanied by high support ticket volumes (+340% above baseline), low task completion rates in the first 60 days post-go-live, and significant productivity loss. Traditional e-learning modules failed to bridge the gap between conceptual knowledge and actual system operation.
03Learning Strategy
▾
Used SAM (Successive Approximation Model) to iterate rapidly with SMEs. Built Whatfix smart walkthroughs for top 20 critical workflows. SAP Enable Now used to create system simulations in Show Me / Try Me / Test Me modes. Camtasia for video demos embedded in LMS as supplementary resources. Blended with live Q&A sessions for complex workflows.
04Learning Experience Architecture
▾
Pre-go-live: Role-based WBT courses on LMS (conceptual foundation). Go-live week: Whatfix walkthroughs activated in-app. Post go-live: Smart tips, performance support overlays, and searchable job aids. Ongoing: LMS tracks completion + Whatfix analytics tracks in-app behavior patterns.
05Operational Outcomes
▾
Support ticket volume reduced by 60% within 45 days. Average task completion time improved by 35%. Proficiency milestone achievement accelerated by 3 weeks vs. previous rollouts. User confidence scores (via post-training survey) increased from 3.1/5 to 4.4/5.

Project 04 · Onboarding / Readiness
Digital Employee Onboarding & Readiness Journey
01Executive Summary
▾
Architected a comprehensive digital onboarding ecosystem that transformed a fragmented 3-day new-hire orientation into a structured 90-day learning journey. The program used AI-assisted support, role-based learning pathways, and readiness assessments to accelerate time-to-productivity and improve new hire retention indicators.
02Business Challenge
▾
New hire time-to-productivity averaged 90+ days due to inconsistent onboarding delivery across managers. 45% of new hires reported feeling "underprepared" at 30-day check-ins. High-volume hiring seasons caused onboarding quality to degrade as L&D resources were stretched thin.
03Learning Experience Architecture
▾
Phase 1 (Day 1–7): Culture, compliance, and system access — Rise 360 WBT modules auto-enrolled on LMS. Phase 2 (Day 8–30): Role-specific skill journeys in Storyline 360 with branching practice scenarios. Phase 3 (Day 31–90): Manager-assigned stretch assignments + AI chatbot support for procedural questions. Readiness gate assessments at Day 30 and Day 60 milestones.
04Stakeholder Ecosystem
▾
HR / HRBPL&DLine Managers OperationsIT / SystemsNew Hires
05Operational Outcomes
▾
Time-to-productivity reduced from 90 days to 58 days (−36%). New hire preparedness scores improved from 55% to 82% at 30-day check-in. Manager satisfaction with onboarding quality increased by 28%. Onboarding consistency achieved regardless of hiring volume or manager availability.

Project 05 · Skills / Tracking
Skills Tracking & Learning Progress Dashboard
01Executive Summary
▾
Developed an integrated skills tracking and learning progress visualization system that mapped individual learner competency growth against organizational capability targets. The system surfaced skill gaps in real-time, enabling L&D teams to prescribe targeted learning interventions and allowing HR leadership to plan capability development with data-backed confidence.
02Business Challenge
▾
HR and L&D had no unified view of workforce skill levels. Performance review data was siloed from training completion data. Succession planning relied on manager intuition rather than evidence-based competency assessment. Skills gap analysis for new business initiatives took 4–6 weeks to compile manually.
03Analytics Layer
▾
Competency framework mapped to course completions via xAPI tags. Power BI connected skills taxonomy to LMS completion, assessment scores, and manager ratings. Dashboard surfaced: skill heat maps by department, individual learner progress bars, competency gap radar charts, and prescribed next-learning recommendations. Basic SQL used for LMS database querying. Excel pivot models used for monthly snapshots.
04LMS & Reporting Ecosystem
▾
Workday (HRIS) → skills tags and role profiles. LMS (Canvas/Moodle) → course completions and assessment scores. xAPI → granular learning activity data. Power BI → visualization layer and self-service reporting. Monthly automated email reports to managers with team skill summaries.
05Operational Outcomes
▾
Skills gap analysis time reduced from 6 weeks to 2 hours. 3 critical capability gaps identified and addressed through targeted learning sprints. L&D spend optimization: 18% of training budget reallocated from low-impact programs to high-gap skills areas. 300+ learners with personalized learning paths driven by skills data.
